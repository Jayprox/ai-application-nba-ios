Privacy Policy for Chalk That NBA

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Chalk That NBA ("the App," "we," "us") is developed and maintained by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Accounts

There is no public sign-up. Accounts are created by the developer. To sign in, the App sends the username and password you enter to our server, which returns sign-in tokens. The tokens are stored in your device's Keychain so you stay signed in; your password is not stored on the device.

Information We Collect

  • Username / account identifier: used to authenticate your account and keep you signed in.
  • Questions you type into Ask: sent to our server to answer them (see "Ask" below).

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, location, health or fitness data, financial or payment information, contacts, photos, or browsing history from outside the App.

Ask (plain-English search)

When you use Ask, the text of your question is sent to our server, which uses Anthropic's Claude model to work out which statistics query to run. The question text is sent to Anthropic for that purpose only. The numbers and the answer sentence come from our own database, not from the model. We keep recent questions briefly to answer repeat questions faster and to enforce usage limits.

How We Use Your Information

The information is used solely for App functionality: signing you in, answering your questions, and keeping the App and its backend secure and reliable. We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, product personalization, or marketing. The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Other than Ask questions being processed by Anthropic as described above, your data stays on our own servers.

Data Retention

We keep your account for as long as it is active. To have your account and associated data deleted, contact us at the address below.

No Real-Money Transactions

Chalk That NBA is a sports research tool. It shows statistics and publicly available sportsbook lines for reference. It does not take wagers, process bets, or handle real-money transactions of any kind, and it does not recommend bets.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
