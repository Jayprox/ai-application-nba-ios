Chalk That Hardwood — Support

Chalk That Hardwood is a pro basketball stats research app: averages, splits, game logs, leaders, standings and sportsbook lines with historical counts. It makes no predictions and takes no wagers.

Accounts

Accounts are by invitation; there is no public sign-up. To delete your account and its data, email the address above.

Common questions

Why isn't a player on the leaders list? To qualify he must have played in at least 70% of his team's games (58 of 82), the league's rule for per-game leaders.

Why do some numbers say "est."? Team ratings, pace and usage rate are estimated from box scores, so they can differ slightly from play-by-play figures.
